01Asli problem kya thi

Project ka pehla crisis: "multimodal fusion" ke paas fuse karne ke liye kuch nahi tha.

Proposal mein teen dataset the — symptom questionnaire, chest X-rays, aur diabetes records. Dikkat yeh thi ki yeh teen alag-alag patients ke groups se aate hain. Ek bhi patient aisa nahi tha jo do dataset mein common ho.

Isliye symptoms X-ray se correlate nahi ho rahe the

Jab patient A ke symptoms aur patient B ka X-ray lete ho, to unke beech koi relation hi nahi hai — model ko sikhne ke liye signal hi nahi milta. Yeh model ki galti nahi thi, data pairing ki galti thi.

02Shared vocabulary ka idea

Do discoveries ne project ko chalaya.

(a) Symptom dataset actually DDXPlus hai

Kaggle ka "disease-symptom-prediction" dataset asal mein DDXPlus research dataset hai: 6,10,000 patients, 223 standardised yes/no clinical questions, aur 49 diagnoses — Pneumonia, Bronchitis, URTI, Influenza, TB, COPD sab included. Original 41-disease toy dataset se bahut richer.

(b) X-ray dataset ke andar missing link mila

Open-I ke har chest X-ray report mein ek "indication" field hoti hai — referring doctor ne scan kyu order kiya, free text mein:

"68-year-old with shortness of breath and weakness. Hypertension. Diabetes."

Humne is text ko parse karke wahi DDXPlus question codes mein convert kiya. Ab har X-ray ke paas usi patient ka symptom vector hai. Yeh hi paired multimodal data hai.

Kyu yeh approach sahi hai

Dono modality ek hi 223-code vocabulary bolte hain. Isliye DDXPlus ke 6.1 lakh patients par trained symptom encoder ko Open-I ke chhote dataset par transfer kiya ja sakta hai — yeh project ka core technical contribution hai.

04Teen bugs jo mile (aur yeh important hai)

Yeh section viva mein kaam aayega — debugging bhi engineering work hai.

Bug 1 — Windows pagefile crash

Training shuru hone se pehle hi mar jaati thi:

OSError: [WinError 1455] The paging file is too small

Kyu: Windows par DataLoader workers spawn hote hain (Linux ki tarah fork nahi). Har worker dobara torch import karta hai aur CUDA DLLs ke liye memory reserve karta hai. 4 workers = 16 GB laptop ki commit limit khatam.

Fix: DEFAULT_WORKERS = 0 if sys.platform == "win32" else 4. Speed par asar nahi — GPU hi bottleneck hai (~27s/epoch).

Bonus problem: 13 orphan processes

Crashed runs exit nahi hue the — 13 python processes 37.5 GB commit charge pakad ke baithe the (working set sirf 2 MB each!). Isliye laptop abnormal lag raha tha aur naya run 512 KB bhi allocate nahi kar paa raha tha. Unhe kill karne par free commit 6 GB → 28.9 GB ho gaya.

Bug 2 — Vocabulary mismatch (yeh sabse khatarnak tha)

size mismatch for symptom_emb.weight:
  checkpoint [231, 256] vs model [233, 256]

Kyu: SymptomEncoder 231 symptoms par pre-trained tha. Baad mein vocabulary expand hui to Open-I 233 par aa gaya.

Easy fix galat hota — yeh samajhna zaroori hai

Seedha tensor "pad" kar dena sabse aasaan tha. Lekin dono vocabulary ka order bhi different tha. Padding karne se pre-trained weights galat symptoms se jud jaate. Model phir bhi train hota, accha-sa number bhi deta — aur humara pura "shared vocabulary" claim chupke se jhooth ho jaata. Yeh woh bug hai jo viva tak zinda reh jaata hai.

Sahi fix: checkpoint ke andar features list stored thi — symptom ke naam. Usse row-by-row remap kiya:

remapped pretrained encoder 231 -> 233 symptoms
  (231 transferred, 2 new: X_RULE_OUT, X_FOLLOWUP)

Verify bhi kiya: 231/231 weights sahi symptom par gaye (0 mismatches), aur 2 naye symptoms fresh initialization par rahe.

Bug 3 — Comparison table mein stale model aa raha tha

compare_openi.py har openi_*_metrics.json ko utha leta tha. Ek purana run (openi_clinicaltext, 578 rows, vocabulary expansion se pehle ka) table mein ghus gaya tha AUC 0.626 ke saath — jo current split se comparable hi nahi tha — aur phir ROC step ko crash kar diya.

Fix: jis run ke rows current test split se match nahi karte, use skip karo aur loudly report karo:

[skipped stale run(s) from a different split]
  - openi_clinicaltext (578 rows vs 519 in current split)

Headline: cross-modal fusion ne kaam kiya ✅

0.8416 vs 0.8216 — fusion ne image-only baseline ko +0.020 AUC se beat kiya, aur 8 findings mein se 4 par sabse accha raha. Dono modality milakar X-ray alone se behtar predict karte hain. Project ka central claim demonstrate ho gaya.

Par ek twist hai — aur yeh chhupana nahi chahiye

Jo fusion pre-training ke saath tha (humara original proposed model), woh 0.8084 — image-only se bhi kam. Matlab DDXPlus pre-training ne madad nahi ki, nuksan kiya.

Kyu (probable): encoder 6.1 lakh DDXPlus patients par trained hai, jahan har patient ke paas bahut saare symptoms hote hain (detailed questionnaire). Open-I mein average 1.35 symptoms hain. Encoder ne jo representation seekha woh dense input expect karta hai — sparse input par woh prior galat hai. Fresh initialization is sparse data ko better adapt karta hai.

Open-I ne de-identify kar diya

Symptoms kahan gaye

Open-I ne publish karne se pehle text ko de-identify kiya — clinical terms ko XXXX se replace kar diya. Actual examples:

"XXXX-year-old XXXX with XXXX."      ← kuch bacha hi nahi
"XXXX, XXXX pain"                    ← body part gayab
"Elevated blood pressure and thoracic pain"  ← yeh parser ki galti thi

Sabse badi dikkat: frequent features hi useless hain

Top features: E_14 (24.1%), X_FEMALE (17.6%), E_66 (17.4%), X_MALE (12.1%).

Top 4 mein se do sex markers hain — jo thoracic findings ke baare mein kuch nahi batate. Jo features clinically useful hain (X_TB_SCREEN 2.7%, X_PAIN_OTHER 3.9%) woh bahut rare hain. Matlab: jo signal available hai woh diagnostic nahi hai.

Isliye clinical branch sirf 0.622 tak pahunchta hai. Fusion phir bhi image-only ko beat karta hai (0.842 vs 0.822) — matlab thoda-sa symptom signal bhi useful hai jab attention use kare. Par margin chhota hai (+0.020) kyunki second modality patli hai. Aur yahi sparsity explain karti hai ki DDXPlus pre-training kyu backfire hua: encoder dense questionnaires (bahut saare symptoms) par trained tha, Open-I deta hai 1.35.

Humne parser improve bhi kiya

296 cases audit kiye jahan real words the par parse empty tha. Rules add kiye: rales, elevated blood pressure, syncopal, nodule, throat pain, headache, chronic airway obstruction.

Ek judgment call jo batana zaroori hai

Sabse bada gap tha bare "pain" (52 cases, jaise "XXXX pain"). DDXPlus ka E_53 literally "pain somewhere" hai — perfect match. Lekin har "pain" ko E_53 map karne se woh 30.9% patients par lag gaya — corpus ka sabse common feature ban jaata, aur E_14 (chest pain) ko duplicate karta.

Isliye use fallback banaya: sirf tab fire hota hai jab koi specific pain site match na ho. Ab prevalence 4.1% — redacted cases recover ho gaye, noise add nahi hua.

Effect: 87 of 296 recovered (baaki irrecoverably redacted), density 1.28 → 1.35, empty vectors 17.8% → 15.2%, aur paired patients 3,105 → 3,459 (11% zyada data). Baseline B 0.5808 → 0.6221 — real gain, par image branch ke 0.82 se door. Phir bhi itna signal kaafi tha ki fusion image-only ko beat kar le.

07Lift 5.2× ka sach (viva mein pooch sakte hain)

"Tumne kaha tha symptoms 5.2× predictive hain. Ab keh rahe ho weak hain. Contradiction?"

Nahi. Dono sach hain, par do alag sawaal ka jawab dete hain.

Lift un patients par measure hui jahan symptom present hai — hypoxia ke liye sirf 33 patients, heart disease ke liye 30.

Ek feature present hone par strongly predictive ho sakta hai, aur phir bhi classifier ke liye useless — agar woh sirf 1% cases mein dikhta hai. Lift batati hai "jab yeh symptom ho to kya hota hai"; classifier ko "har patient ke liye" predict karna padta hai.

08Viva ke sawaal (aur jawab)

"DDXPlus par 99.34% accuracy? Yeh too good to be true hai."

Bilkul sahi observation, aur hum khud yeh likhte hain. DDXPlus synthetic dataset hai — clinical knowledge base se generate hua hai, isliye symptoms aur diagnosis ke beech relation almost deterministic hai. Woh number batata hai pipeline correct hai, model clinically accha hai yeh nahi. Project ka honest test Open-I par hai — real radiographs, real noisy text, aur wahan numbers 0.62–0.82 hain.

"Pre-training ne nuksan kiya — to tumhara shared-vocabulary contribution fail hua?"

Yeh sabse sharp sawaal hai. Honest jawab do hisson mein:

(1) Shared vocabulary zaroori thi. Uske bina koi bhi fusion model exist hi nahi karta — teen datasets mein ek bhi common patient nahi tha. Paired data banane ka mechanism yahi hai, aur fusion (0.842 > 0.822) usi paired data par jeeta hai.

(2) Pre-trained weights transfer karna madad nahi kiya — 0.808 vs 0.842. Yeh ek alag claim hai, aur yeh humne khud ablation se detect kiya. Wajah measurable hai: DDXPlus dense questionnaires deta hai, Open-I 1.35 symptoms. Transfer learning tab kaam karta hai jab source aur target distribution match karein; yahan nahi karte.

Toh contribution (vocabulary) stands, specific technique (weight transfer) ne is corpus par kaam nahi kiya. Dono alag se report karna hi sahi science hai.

"Sirf 519 test patients — numbers reliable hain?"

Macro AUC reasonably stable hai, par rare findings noisy hain: edema_congestion ke sirf 17 test cases hain, chest_infection ke 27. Isliye hum macro AUC par comparison karte hain, individual rare-class F1 par nahi. Yeh limitation hum khud likhte hain — defend karne ki koshish nahi karte.

"Clinical use ke liye ready hai?"

Nahi, aur yeh har page par likha hai. Yeh decision support research prototype hai, diagnosis nahi. Qualified medical review zaroori hai. 519 patients par 0.82 AUC clinical deployment ke liye bahut door hai.

09Aage kya kar sakte hain

  1. Behtar paired dataset: MIMIC-CXR mein full radiology reports + structured clinical data hai, aur de-identification itni aggressive nahi. Yeh sabse high-impact next step hai.
  2. Impression/findings text use karo: humne sirf indication field use ki. Baaki report text mein zyada signal ho sakta hai (par careful — woh radiologist ka output hai, label leakage ka risk hai).
  3. Pre-training strategy badlo (sabse interesting lead): pure weight transfer ne nuksan kiya. Behtar options: encoder ko Open-I ki sparsity par fine-tune karo, ya DDXPlus patients ko artificially sparsify karke pre-train karo (sirf 1-2 symptoms rakh ke), taaki source distribution target se match kare.
  4. Multiple seeds / cross-validation chalao: +0.020 ka gain single split par hai. 5 seeds par mean ± std report karna claim ko bahut strong banayega. Yeh sabse sasta aur sabse zyada credibility dene wala step hai.
  5. Image branch freeze karke fusion tune karo: fusion (pre) epoch 10 par early-stop hua — overfitting ka signal. 2,421 training cases 9M-parameter model ke liye kam hain.
  6. Cardiac findings par focus: baseline B cardiomegaly (0.700) aur edema_congestion (0.743) par theek karta hai, opacity (0.528) par nahi. Clinically coherent hai — "CHF" likhne wala doctor cardiac failure describe kar raha hai, jabki opacity ka koi distinctive presenting symptom nahi hota. Sirf cardiac subset par fusion zyada promising ho sakta hai.
